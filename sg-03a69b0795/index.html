Friday: land and get ready

Friday 4 December. You are on your own until dinner.

  1. Midday

    Land at Changi

    Take the MRT towards the city and change at Tanah Merah, or take a taxi or Grab. Get off at Bugis, which is the station for your hotel.

  2. 13:30

    Check in at the hotel

    Check-in starts at 14:00. If you arrive early, ask them to keep your bag. Bring your passport. They take a SGD 100 deposit at check-in, and a card is easiest. I will send you the hotel name and address separately.

  3. Afternoon

    Collect your race pack

    Collection is open until 22:00 on Friday. The venue was not published yet when I wrote this, so check the race website and I will send it when I have it. Take the QR code from your race receipt.

  4. 19:00

    Dinner at Tekka Centre

    I will meet you here. It has several vegetarian Indian stalls in Little India. Open on the map

  5. 20:15

    Mustafa Centre

    A huge shop with spices, sweets, gifts and electronics at low prices. It is a few minutes' walk from Farrer Park MRT and is reported to open late or all night. Keep it to about 90 minutes, because your race is in two days. Open on the map

    Tourist GST refund: ask at the till whether they give refund tickets, and keep your receipts. See Wednesday.

  6. 21:45

    Back at the hotel

    Early night.

Monday: Merlion and Marina Bay

Monday 7 December. You are on your own until 19:00. Your legs will be stiff, so this day has plenty of sitting.

  1. 09:30

    The Merlion

    Merlion Park is free and open at all hours. The nearest MRT station is Raffles Place, about a 10-minute walk. Take your medal for the photo. Have lunch nearby. Open on the map

  2. 13:00

    Rest at the hotel and change

    Please do not skip this. Tonight's restaurant has a dress code, and you will want a shower and dry clothes.

  3. 16:00

    Gardens by the Bay

    Take the Downtown Line from Bugis to Bayfront. It is a direct ride. The Cloud Forest and Flower Dome conservatories are next door, with seats and shelter if it rains. Check the opening hours on the day. Open on the map

  4. 18:45

    Walk to Marina Bay Sands

    It is connected to the Bayfront station area. Open on the map

  5. 19:00

    Dinner at CÉ LA VI

    I will meet you at Marina Bay Sands. It is the rooftop restaurant and bar. Dress smart casual. One listing says no singlets, swimwear, sleeveless tops or flip-flops after 17:30. Open on the map

    I will book the table. Tell me about any foods you avoid, for example fish sauce or stock, so I can ask them about vegetarian dishes.

  6. 21:00

    The light and water show

    Spectra is free and is reported to run at 20:00 and 21:00, from the Event Plaza in front of the shops. Arrive early for a good spot. The times still need checking.

  7. 22:30

    Back at the hotel

    We will decide on Tuesday tonight, once you know how your legs feel: Universal Studios or the Oceanarium. Then we book the tickets.

08

Tuesday: Sentosa and the rides

Tuesday 8 December. I have the day off, so we go together. Both choices below are at Resorts World Sentosa, so we can decide on Monday night.

  1. 09:00

    Meet at the hotel and travel to Sentosa

    I will meet you at the hotel. From Bugis take the East-West Line to Outram Park, then the North-East Line to HarbourFront. Then ride the Sentosa Express monorail from VivoCity to Resorts World. Allow 30 to 45 minutes.

  2. 10:00

    Option A: Universal Studios Singapore

    A full day of rides. The park is usually open 10:00 to 20:00, but December hours need checking. Wear comfortable shoes and take a poncho. An Express Pass shortens the time you stand in queues, and that matters with sore legs. Open on the map

    If it storms or your legs say no, switch to option B on the day.

  3. 18:00

    Option A: head back

    Leave the park around 18:00 so you are in Bugis by about 19:30.

  4. 19:30

    Option A: early dinner in Bugis

    Fortune Centre and Fu Lu Shou Complex are both reported to have vegetarian restaurants. Pack afterwards and sleep. Open Fortune Centre on the map

  5. 10:00

    Option B: Singapore Oceanarium

    Indoors, air-conditioned and mostly seated, about 2 to 3 hours. It is the former S.E.A. Aquarium, rebuilt and renamed. Lunch at Resorts World, where I will check what is vegetarian. Open on the map

    One listing says it is closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. I will check before we choose.

  6. 14:30

    Option B: Chinatown

    From HarbourFront the North-East Line goes straight to Chinatown. Visit the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, which is free, and cover your shoulders and knees. Then the Sri Mariamman Temple, and the Chinatown Heritage Centre if you like museums. It is reported open until 20:00 but closes one day a month for maintenance, so we will check the date. Open the temple on the map

  7. 18:00

    Option B: dinner at Maxwell Food Centre

    A big hawker centre. Some stalls are reported to serve vegetarian dishes, but I have not confirmed which. Ask for vegetarian and check about egg and fish sauce. The Downtown Line goes straight back to Bugis. Open on the map

Good to know

Getting around

  • The MRT is the easiest way across the city. Bugis is on the East-West and Downtown lines.
  • A contactless bank card is reported to work at the MRT gates. Check that yours does, and keep some Singapore dollars for hawker stalls.
  • Grab is the local taxi app. Set it up before you leave home.

Eating vegetarian

  • Little India has the most Indian vegetarian food: Komala Vilas and Tekka Centre.
  • Fortune Centre and Fu Lu Shou Complex, near the hotel, have vegetarian restaurants.
  • Ask about shrimp paste, fish sauce and lard. They can hide in dishes that look vegetarian.

Weather

December is usually the wettest month, and it stays warm and humid. Carry a compact umbrella or poncho. Check haze.gov.sg and the race organiser's channels in race week, because the organiser has said it has plans for haze.
